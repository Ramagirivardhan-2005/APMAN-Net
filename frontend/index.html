Optical Model (Jaffe-McGlamery Formulation)

Underwater degradation is physically modeled as: I_λ(x) = J_λ(x) • t_λ(x) + A_λ • (1 - t_λ(x)). APMAN-Net adaptively estimates channel-dependent medium transmission t(x) and backscatter light A to invert degradation directly.

Dual Attention & Edge Gating

Features pass through CBAM (Channel & Spatial Attention) combined with Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) and a Dual Sobel-Laplacian Edge-Gated module to eliminate halos while preserving fine coral and marine textures.

4K Ultra HD Detail Fusion

Restored RGB outputs are transformed to CIE LAB space, fusing neural chromaticity predictions with high-frequency unsharp edge details to output crisp 3840 × 2160 images without artifacting.
